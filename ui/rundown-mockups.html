The fusion logic
Apple owns the chrome — layout, typography, cards, pills, frosted glass. Sony owns the instrument — the LCD deck with its glow, scanlines, and embossed transport is the one skeuomorphic object in an otherwise flat system. One hero texture, everything else quiet.
Desktop = producer
Sidebar navigation, full topic list, filters, generate. The Walkman deck lives docked in the sidebar corner — always visible, always in reach, like a hardware remote sitting on the desk.
Mobile = remote control
The deck is the hero — first thing under the title, thumb-reachable transport buttons. Topic list below is "up next." This is the couch/phone view over Tailscale: check topics in the morning, drive the show from the deck at night.
